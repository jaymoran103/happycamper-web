HappyCamper

Catch schedule problems before your campers do

Camp schedules balance complex constraints, and the final check falls to the busiest camp leaders on their busiest day of the session.

HappyCamper helps those leaders efficiently catch oversights the night before activities begin. With flexible configurations and purpose-built filters, leaders can quickly make sense of their data, and make informed revisions that prioritize camper satisfaction and rounded schedules.
